# 📞 Telecom Megaline Phone Plan Analysis & Statistical Data Analysis

## 📌 Executive Summary
This project analyzes customer usage and revenue data for two telecom phone plans to evaluate profitability and customer behavior. Statistical hypothesis testing was conducted to support data-driven pricing and product strategy decisions.

---

## 🧩 Problem Statement
Telecom companies must understand customer behavior and plan profitability to optimize pricing strategies. This project compares two phone plans to determine which plan generates higher revenue and how customer usage patterns differ.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Python (pandas, NumPy, SciPy)
- Data Visualization (matplotlib, seaborn)
- Statistical Analysis and Hypothesis Testing

---

## 🔍 Methodology
1. Data cleaning and preprocessing  
2. Exploratory data analysis of call, text, and internet usage  
3. Revenue calculation per user and plan  
4. Statistical hypothesis testing (t-tests)  
5. Business insight generation and recommendations  

---

## 📈 Key Findings
- Identified differences in revenue and usage patterns between phone plans  
- Statistical testing confirmed significant differences in plan profitability  
- Usage behavior varied across customer segments and regions  

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Adjust pricing strategies based on profitability differences  
- Target high-value customer segments with tailored plans  
- Use behavioral insights to optimize marketing and retention strategies
